contextual_request: A post that asks for a recommendation or solution and supplies at least two decision-relevant details, such as player count, age, time, complexity, budget, previous games, or preferred mechanics.
supported_recommendation: A post recommends or discourages a game and gives at least one concrete reason connected to the reader's needs, such as player count, duration, mechanics, complexity, interaction, or observed play experience.
bare_recommendation: A post recommends or discourages a game but provides no concrete reason beyond its title, agreement, or generic praise such as "great," "fun," or "the best."
experience_story: A post primarily recounts a personal event, reaction, or memory about playing a game and does not principally ask for or provide a recommendation.
